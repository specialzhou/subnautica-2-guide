# Subnautica 2 — Tadpole Build Guide: Chassis, Depth Modules & Recipes

> A quick-start reference for the Tadpole, Subnautica 2's modular starter vehicle.
> **Early Access note:** numbers below come from the Subnautica 2 Wiki (CC BY-NC-SA) and may shift as the game develops — always sanity-check in-game. Full source-linked data + an interactive calculator live at the link in section 5.

## 1. Unlocking the Tadpole

The Tadpole is built at the **Vehicle Fabricator** (unlocked by scanning its blueprint fragments scattered through the early biomes). Once the fabricator is placed, you pick one of three **chassis**, then optionally bolt on **depth modules** at a **Modification Station**.

- Scan wreckage / fragments → unlock the Vehicle Fabricator blueprint.
- Build the Vehicle Fabricator (it crafts the chassis directly — no separate blueprint per chassis).
- Craft a **chassis** → your first Tadpole.
- (Optional) Craft **depth modules** at the Modification Station to go deeper.

## 2. The three chassis

All three are built at the **Vehicle Fabricator**. Pick based on how you want to explore.

| Chassis | Speed | Fragments | Crafting inputs | Best for |
|---------|-------|-----------|----------------|----------|
| **Base** | 8 | 3 | 2× Titanium Ingot, 1× Glass, 1× System Chip, 1× Power Cell | Balanced all-rounder; cheapest, easiest first ride |
| **Haul** | 8 | 3 | 4× Titanium Ingot, 3× Strontium, 3× Enameled Glass, 1× Dedicated Core | Cargo / storage focus (heavy build) |
| **Scout** | 10 | 1 | 2× Plasteel Ingot, 1× Advanced Wiring Kit, 1× Dedicated Core, 1× Strong Acid | Fast recon; fewest fragments to unlock |

**Base** is the sensible first build — low fragment count, cheap inputs, and it gets you mobile fast. **Scout** is the speed option if you just want to zip around and only needs a single fragment. **Haul** is the resource-hauler variant when you're moving a lot of materials.

> Base chassis depth without modules: **250 m**. Depth modules below extend that.

## 3. Depth modules (Modification Station)

Built at the **Modification Station**, not the Vehicle Fabricator. Stack both for maximum depth.

| Module | Depth added | Crafting inputs |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| **Depth Module Mk I** | +200 m | 3× Celestine, 2× Enameled Glass, 1× System Chip |
| **Depth Module Mk II** | +550 m | 2× Dedicated Core, 2× Troilite, 2× Mangalloy Ingot |

Combined with the base 250 m, two modules take the Tadpole well into the deeper biomes.

## 4. Which to build first?

- **Just starting out → Base chassis.** Cheapest, 3 fragments, gets you exploring immediately.
- **Need speed → Scout chassis.** One fragment, fastest (speed 10).
- **Hauling base materials → Haul chassis.** More input cost, storage-oriented.
- **Going deep → add Mk I, then Mk II** at the Modification Station once you have the rarer materials (Dedicated Core, Troilite, Mangalloy).
