Current answer

Add more generation: Solar Panels give 8 energy each during daytime above 200m (about a quarter of that at night), so stack several. For steady output build a Bioreactor — it burns biological matter for 10 energy in Normal mode (fragments at Quaker's Meetinghouse, the Empty Hold, or scattered debris). Also check depth: solars fade below 200m.

Evidence boundary

Output figures are Wiki-backed (Solar Panel rev 23423, Bioreactor rev 23448, both 2026-09-14). Which appliance is draining the base cannot be known from the post; adding generation is the documented fix.
