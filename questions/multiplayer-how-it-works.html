Current answer

Yes — Subnautica 2 has optional online co-op for up to 4 players, and solo play is fully supported. Multiplayer is cross-platform. Hosting is currently peer-to-peer only; dedicated servers are not available yet. Joining, hosting and save behavior beyond that are not officially documented.
