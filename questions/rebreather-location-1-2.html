Did they move the rebreather in 1.2?

↑
0
↳

Current answer

It was not moved — the Rebreather blueprint still unlocks by scanning one Rebreather. Documented locations: a Colonist Bunker in the Dolerite Spires, and the Lander Garage wreck (bring a Repair Tool). It counters the oxygen drain when diving below 100m.

Evidence boundary

Unlock method and locations are Wiki-backed (Rebreather, rev 24575, 2026-10-06). The page lists additional fragment coordinates; the two above are the documented ones relevant here.
